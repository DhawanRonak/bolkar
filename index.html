TAKE BOLKAR TO YOUR FORM

Install in Chrome.

  1. Download this repository and find the extension folder.
  2. Open chrome://extensions. Turn on Developer mode.
  3. Choose Load unpacked and select that folder.
  4. Open a Google Form, then click the BolKar extension icon.

The extension fills the open form. You review it and press Submit yourself.
